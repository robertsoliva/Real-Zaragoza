Scout Verdict

Radunović is an attacking-minded RB with a demonstrably high ceiling — his 8-assist season (62837) is the highest single-season output of any RB candidate in this pool, and his 1.77 KP/90 in the primary season confirms he remains a consistent chance creator even in a down year. The FCSB context matters: this is the Romanian champion, playing expansive, possession-heavy football (60.3% avg possession), which inflates both opportunity and output for wide defenders. The year-on-year rating drop (7.19 → 6.88) and the halving of interceptions/90 (1.31 → 0.77) are genuine flags, not noise — the data points to either a tactical shift by FCSB (higher defensive line, less counter-press exposure) or a player who had one exceptional season. Fouls committed also fell (34 → 20), which could reflect reduced defensive engagement or improved discipline. This ambiguity is the core risk. As a backup to Gabilondo the financial exposure is low, but the scout should seek live video before moving to contract stage.
