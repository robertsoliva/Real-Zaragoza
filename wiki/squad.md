## Context

The squad is being rebuilt almost from scratch for life in **Primera RFEF** (third tier, semi-professional) under new head coach **Ibai Gómez** (see [current-situation.md](./current-situation.md)). Primera RFEF's financial fair-play rules impose a much lower salary ceiling than Segunda División, which is the direct cause of the scale of turnover described below — several players who were good enough for Segunda are reportedly unaffordable in the new category. Sporting director **Lalo Arantegui** has said he wants 80%+ of the squad settled before the first day of preseason training on 20 July 2026.

## 2026–27 captaincy

The club used a four-captain system in 2025–26: Francho Serrano, Keidi Bare, Raúl "Guti," and Iván Calero. Two of those are gone (Guti out of contract; Bare/Calero status pending). **Francho Serrano renewed through 2030** (announced during a tribute act at La Romareda; confirmed on official site) and is the natural dressing-room leader going into the rebuild.

**Open item:** formal 2026–27 captaincy announcement not yet made.

- **Loan out confirmed (post-July 7):** **Iker García** — loan to Arenas de Getxo. (source: official site, 2026-07)
- **At risk of release on salary-cap grounds — reported only, not verified against a source of truth:** Dani Gómez, Valery, Keidi Bare. No realzaragoza.com or Wikipedia confirmation found either way; treat as rumour.
- **Sold — confirmed:** **Samed Baždar** — to **Sint-Truidense** (Belgian Jupiler Pro League). Confirmed directly on realzaragoza.com: joined summer 2024, 40 matches / 5 goals / 5 assists for the club, spent the second half of last season on loan at Jagiellonia (Poland), represented Bosnia & Herzegovina at FIFA World Cup 2026. - Sporting director Lalo Arantegui has been reported as targeting a total of **26 departures** — this figure is press-only (not on realzaragoza.com/Wikipedia); treat as a rumoured/approximate scale, not a confirmed count.

## Players returning from loan (futures unclear)

Two players regarded among the most successful loanees last season are back at the club with uncertain roles: **Adrián Liso**, **Pau Sans** (a third, Samed Baždar, has since been sold — see Confirmed departures above, confirmed on the official site). Press describes their outlooks as differing — don't assume both are kept.

**Adrián Liso** — no realzaragoza.com or Wikipedia article confirms a departure; as of 2026-08-06 he is still training with the squad and expected in the Andorra friendly. Everything below this line is **rumour only, sourced entirely from El Desmarque, not independently confirmed**: that his exit is a stated Arantegui priority to fund a CF/CB signing, an asking price dropping from ~€5M to ~€3M+sell-on, and interest from Sevilla/Alavés/Espanyol/Valencia/Venezia/Mallorca.

## Confirmed 2026 summer signings (so far)

15 arrivals confirmed as of 2026-07-22. Includes 4 returning loanees (Baždar, Pau Sans, Adrián Liso, Hugo Carrillo) whose futures were previously uncertain — all now confirmed as part of the squad.

- **Sem Westerveld** — goalkeeper, born 18 Jul 2002 (21), Dutch. Free transfer from MVV Maastricht (Eerste Divisie). 3-year contract. AZ Alkmaar academy product; played all 38 league matches for MVV in 2025-26 (155 saves, 6 clean sheets, avg 4+ saves/match); 79 professional appearances total; 1.92 m. UEFA Youth League experience (2021-22, 6 apps, 84% save%). (confirmed via official site, 2026-07)
- **Anartz Peña** — goalkeeper. Free transfer from Arenas de Getxo.
- **Jaume Jardí** — Free transfer from Nàstic.
- **Rubén Díez ("Jamelli")** — Free transfer from AD Ceuta.
- **Raúl Pereira** — Transfer from Cádiz.
- **Peter Ademo** — midfielder, born 11 Jan 2003 (23), Nigerian. From Sheriff Tiraspol (Moldovan Super Liga). Formerly at Rapid București (loan, Romanian SuperLiga 24/25 — 11 apps, 230 mins) and A Lyga Lithuania (2023). Europa League group-stage experience with Sheriff (23/24). Market value €360k.
- **Sergio Escudero** — left back, free transfer from RC Deportivo de A Coruña (who were promoted to Primera División), 1-year deal. Experienced defender, born in Valladolid. (confirmed via official site, 2026-07)
- **Jokin Gabilondo** — right back, born 9 Apr 1999 (25). Free transfer from Málaga CF. 2-year contract. Real Sociedad academy product; helped Real Sociedad B promotion in 2022-23 (37 apps, 4G 1A), then Málaga promotion to LaLiga Hypermotion (37 apps) — two promotions in three years. (confirmed via official site, 2026-07)
- **Emil Hansson** — left winger, born in Bergen (Norway), 28 years old. Free transfer from Excelsior (Eerste Divisie, Netherlands). 2-year contract (until June 2028). Swedish/Norwegian dual eligibility — represented Sweden in youth categories. Career: 300+ professional appearances across Norway, Netherlands, England, Germany. Best season: Heracles 2022-23 (Dutch 2nd div) — 16 goals + 18 assists, earned club promotion. Most recent: Birmingham City (English 3rd tier, 24 apps, 2G 2A) then Excelsior loan (1G 1A). Announced ~2026-07-10 via official site.
- **Edu Espiau** — center forward. Free transfer from Arka Gdynia (Polish Ekstraklasa). 2-year contract. Canary Islands-born; built career in Spanish Segunda División (Las Palmas, Ponferradina, Burgos CF). Moved to Poland (Arka Gdynia) in summer 2025, scored 6 goals. Announced 2026-07-11; described at the time as the club's "10th signing." Fills the CF gap identified as top market priority.
- **Ander Herrera** — midfielder, born 14 Aug 1989 (turning 37). Free transfer from Boca Juniors. 1-year contract (through June 2027). Aragonese by heritage; Real Zaragoza academy product before departing summer 2011. Career: Athletic Club → Manchester United → PSG → Athletic Club (2023) → Boca Juniors (Jan 2025). Injury-plagued at Boca (29 matches in 17 months). Returns to the club of his formation as a squad leader figure; knows head coach Ibai Gómez from their time together at Athletic Club. Fan reaction mixed — controversy about his age and physical state, though majority expressed solidarity. Announced ~2026-07-14; in training from July 20.
- **Diego González** — central defender (versatile: can play as LCB, left back, or defensive midfielder). Born 7 May 1999 (25), from Villarreal. Free transfer from AD Ceuta. 2-year contract (+ 1-year option). Betis youth academy product; senior career at Andorra FC, SD Huesca, then Ceuta. ~100 LaLiga Hypermotion appearances across three seasons; 35 apps for Ceuta in 2024-25. Left-footed. Fills the defensive depth need confirmed by Lalo Arantegui (min. 2 CBs needed). Announced 2026-07-20-21 — described as the club's "12th signing."
